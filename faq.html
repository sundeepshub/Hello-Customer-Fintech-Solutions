Frequently asked questions

Simple answers before you start.

If your question is not listed here, you can start an enquiry and tell us what you are trying to understand.

Do you guarantee loan approval?

No. Approval is decided by the respective lender after its own eligibility and credit assessment.

Can I choose a preferred bank?

Yes. The enquiry can record your preferred bank or lender. If you have no preference, you can ask us to discuss suitable options where available.

Do I need to know my CIBIL score?

No. If you do not know it, say so. If you have a credit concern, the separate CIBIL & Credit page lets you provide the account-level details.

Can I enquire if I already have EMIs?

Yes. Tell us about your existing obligations accurately so the requirement can be understood in context.

Can self-employed people enquire?

Yes. The form asks different questions depending on employment/business profile and the selected requirement.

What documents will I need?

Documents vary by loan type and customer profile. The enquiry shows relevant supporting-document categories rather than assuming the same checklist for everyone.

What is Project Funds?

It is a separate enquiry route for projects such as real estate, hospitals, building construction, godowns/warehouses and other eligible project requirements.

Can I refer another person?

Yes. Use the separate Refer Someone page and provide only information you are comfortable sharing for the stated purpose.

Will my enquiry automatically become a loan application?

No. An enquiry is a request to discuss your requirement. Any formal application and lender consent process is separate.

Are interest rates and charges fixed?

No. They depend on the lender, product, profile, credit assessment and current terms.

Can I request loan consolidation or credit-card clearance?

Yes. These are included as enquiry options where relevant; the final product and eligibility depend on the lender.

Can I ask about agriculture loans?

Yes. Agriculture Loans are included as a requirement option. The relevant lender and product criteria will determine suitability.

Is my information kept private?

We use the information submitted to respond to the stated enquiry and handle it according to the website's privacy terms and applicable requirements.

Can I edit my enquiry before submitting?

Yes. The enquiry flow includes a review stage so you can check and edit information before final submission.

Can I contact you without filling the complete form?

Phone and WhatsApp contact options can remain available. For a detailed enquiry, the form helps organise the information needed for a useful discussion.

Who makes the final lending decision?

The respective lender makes the final decision under its applicable credit, risk, documentation and policy requirements.
