Application Configuration & Admin Responsibilities

This page documents and controls the platform items that require administrator ownership.

Telecaller & Connector management

Approve pending signup, activate/deactivate accounts, review role/Executive ID, assign/reassign leads, and ensure users see only their own/assigned leads.

Lead operations

Review unassigned web enquiries, allocate leads, monitor stage/status, follow-ups, conversions, documents and closures.

Rates & lender content

Verify lender rates from official sources, update ranges/fees/conditions, record Last Updated, and control role visibility.

Schemes & subsidies

Maintain scheme categories, official links, eligibility, benefits, Do's/Don'ts and last-verification notes. Control public/executive visibility.

Security & access

Maintain Firebase Authentication, Firestore/Storage rules, Cloud Functions, Admin account, password-reset templates and least-privilege access.

Reporting & Google Sheets

Maintain the master lead Sheet and provision one reporting spreadsheet per approved executive. Credentials/passwords must never be stored in Sheets.

Profile photos

Stored in Firebase Storage under profiles/{uid}/.... Admin should monitor file type/size policy. Do not store profile photos in the public GitHub repository.

Backups & audit

Export/backup business data periodically, keep change logs, deactivate exited users promptly, and review access/rules after every major release.

Production prerequisites: Firebase Email/Password Authentication enabled; Firestore created; Storage enabled; Firestore and Storage rules deployed; Cloud Functions deployed; authorised domains configured; Google Apps Script web app deployed; first Admin bootstrapped; Firebase email templates checked; executive reporting sheet sync configured if required.
