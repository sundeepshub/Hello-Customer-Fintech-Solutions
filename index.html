FAQ

Common questions

Does completing the form guarantee a loan?

No. It is only an enquiry and preliminary screening. The respective lender decides eligibility, rate, amount, tenure and approval.

Will you ask for OTPs or banking passwords?

No. Never provide OTPs, PINs, passwords or other confidential banking credentials through this website or to anyone claiming to represent us.

Can I enquire about more than one loan?

Yes. Submit a separate enquiry for each requirement, or mention the additional requirement in the message field.

What happens after I submit?

Your enquiry can be reviewed by our team, who may contact you for clarification and discuss the next steps.
