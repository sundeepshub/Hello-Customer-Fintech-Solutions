Why customers reach out

Real problems. Practical conversations.

These are real-world situations customers commonly discuss with us. Every case is reviewed on its own facts; lender decisions remain independent.

“I have too many EMIs.”

We map each EMI, outstanding amount and tenure before discussing whether consolidation or a transfer may be relevant.

“My CIBIL history has an issue.”

We first understand the account, the nature of the issue, current overdue status and when it started.

“My loan application was rejected.”

We can review the information and documents available before discussing possible next steps. A lender still makes the final decision.

“I have an overdue account.”

Tell us which account has the overdue and whether it is currently outstanding so the situation is clear.

“I have multiple credit-card dues.”

We can record the obligations separately and understand whether you are exploring a consolidation option.

“I am not sure which loan I need.”

Start with the purpose and requirement; the enquiry flow then asks loan-specific questions.

“I recently changed jobs.”

We capture current and total experience so the employment situation can be understood.

“I am salaried but do not have business documents.”

That is okay: salaried applicants are not asked for business registration, GST returns or audited financials.

“I am self-employed.”

We ask about business vintage, turnover, banking and the proofs you can provide.

“My business does not have all registrations.”

We can discuss applicable registration or renewal support and what may be relevant to your business.

“I do not have GST returns.”

The form will ask about GST based on the selected loan and your profile rather than assuming every customer has it.

“I need a home loan for a resale property.”

Property type, confirmation status and relevant property details are captured before the enquiry is reviewed.

“I want to transfer my home loan.”

The form captures current lender, outstanding amount, EMI, remaining tenure and possible top-up requirement.

“I want to reduce my EMI burden.”

We capture existing commitments first so the discussion starts with the actual numbers.

“I need a loan for business expansion.”

Business type, vintage, turnover and supporting documents help structure the enquiry.

“I am buying a vehicle.”

New/used status and vehicle-specific details are collected to keep the enquiry relevant.

“I need education funding abroad.”

Country, course, institution, intake and estimated education cost can be captured.

“My address proof is not available.”

You can state that clearly and explain the situation rather than entering inaccurate information.

“I do not know my CIBIL score.”

You can select that the score is unknown; a known score is not mandatory just to explain a concern.

“I have more than one CIBIL issue.”

The form asks how many affected accounts you have and then creates a separate issue section for each.

“I want to speak to someone.”

You can request a meeting, choose a mode and select a preferred date. Phone and WhatsApp remain available.

“I am available for a weekend meeting.”

For Saturday or Sunday, the form also asks for a preferred time.

“I need documents organised before applying.”

We can help you understand which documents may be relevant to the selected loan and profile.

“I want to refer a friend or customer.”

Use the referral section to select the service required and provide the referrer and referred person's details.

“I need private finance.”

The referral flow can record whether the requirement is collateral-based or non-collateral-based and capture additional details.

A more human way to handle finance

About Sandeep

Hello Customer Fintech Solutions is built around one simple idea: understand the customer first, then discuss the possible solution.

Sandeep believes a financial enquiry should never make a customer feel rushed, judged or confused. Every person's income, commitments, business situation, documents, property requirement and credit history can be different. That is why the approach is to listen carefully, ask relevant questions and organise the information before discussing possible options.

The service is built around listening, organising, explaining, respecting and coordinating. Customers should know why a document is being requested, what information is still missing and which decisions belong to the respective lender or service provider.

Customer information is treated with care. The website is designed to avoid unnecessary collection of sensitive authentication information, and customers are specifically advised never to share OTPs, passwords, card PINs or net-banking credentials.

There is no pressure to proceed simply because an enquiry was submitted. If a requirement is not suitable, the conversation should remain honest. If more information is needed, we explain what is missing. If a lender makes the final decision, we make that distinction clear.

Respect is part of the service: every customer deserves a patient conversation, clear communication and the opportunity to make an informed decision.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does the form guarantee loan approval?

No. It is an enquiry and information-gathering flow. Final approval and terms are decided by the respective lender.

Why are you asking for existing EMI details?

Understanding each EMI can help organise your current commitments and the reason for considering a transfer or consolidation.

Can I request a meeting?

Yes. Select the meeting option and your preferred mode/time. The request is captured with your enquiry so a suitable conversation can be arranged.

Can I send documents by email?

Yes. After submission, you receive a thank-you message with instructions to send relevant documents to hellocustomerfirst@gmail.com.

Will you show my internal lead priority?

No. Any internal lead-priority score is for internal workflow only and is not displayed to the customer and is not a credit score.

What happens if I do not have a current address proof?

Select “No current address proof” and provide the reason. The team can then understand the documentation situation before discussing next steps.
