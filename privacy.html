Privacy Policy

Hello Customer Fintech Solutions respects the information you provide through this website. This page explains the general way enquiry information is collected and used.

Information collected

Depending on your enquiry, the form may collect contact information, date of birth, address information, employment/income information, loan requirement details, existing EMI details, documentation availability, property/business/vehicle/education details, CIBIL-related information and meeting preferences.

Purpose

The information is used to understand your enquiry, contact you, organise relevant documentation and discuss possible next steps with you. Information may be shared with a lender or service provider only where appropriate and subject to applicable consent, law and the relevant lender's process.

Data minimisation and consent

Do not enter OTPs, passwords, card PINs, net-banking passwords or other authentication credentials into this website. The form uses consent controls for customer communication.

Third-party services

The website may use a postal/pincode lookup service to retrieve postal metadata. Google Sheets/Apps Script may be used as the internal lead-record system when the enquiry is submitted.

Retention and requests

Information may be retained for enquiry handling, documentation and business records, subject to applicable requirements. For privacy questions or requests, contact hellocustomerfirst@gmail.com.

Important

This website is an enquiry and assistance interface. It does not itself sanction or approve loans. Final eligibility, terms and approval are determined by the respective lender.
