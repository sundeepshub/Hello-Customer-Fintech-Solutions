Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 5 September 2026

Important: Hello Customer Fintech Solutions is an enquiry, assistance and coordination platform. We do not represent that we are a bank, NBFC, credit bureau or RBI-regulated lender unless a specific relationship is expressly disclosed for a particular transaction.

1. Scope

This policy explains how information submitted through this website, Guest forms, referral forms and authorised portal workflows may be collected, used, shared, protected and retained.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the service requested, this may include name, mobile number, email, date of birth, address/pincode, employment or business information, income, loan requirement, existing loans/EMIs, credit-card obligations, CIBIL/credit concerns, document availability, property/vehicle/education/project information, meeting preferences, referral information and insurance-enquiry information. We do not ask you to submit OTPs, passwords, card PINs, CVVs or net-banking credentials.

3. Why we use information

We use information to respond to the enquiry, understand the requirement, organise documents, arrange follow-up, maintain lead/service records, provide requested calculators or assistance, prevent misuse, and coordinate with a relevant lender/service provider when appropriate.

4. Consent and choice

Where consent is requested, it should be specific to the stated enquiry or communication purpose. You may ask us to stop non-essential follow-up. Where applicable, you may request correction, updating or deletion, subject to legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention and legitimate record-keeping requirements.

5. Sharing

Information may be shared with banks, NBFCs, insurers or other service providers only where relevant to the service requested and subject to applicable consent and their own eligibility, underwriting, privacy and compliance processes. We do not sell personal information as a customer list.

6. Technology and processors

The website may use Firebase Authentication for authorised portal access, Google Apps Script/Google Sheets/Drive for internal workflows and records, and pincode/postal lookup services. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical controls appropriate to the application. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Never send authentication credentials or payment-card secrets through an enquiry form, email, chat or WhatsApp.

8. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for enquiry handling, follow-up, service records, legal/compliance needs, dispute handling or fraud prevention. Retention periods may differ by record type and provider requirements.

9. Your requests

For access, correction, consent withdrawal, deletion or another privacy request, email hellocustomerfirst@gmail.com. We may need to verify the requester before acting.

10. Children

This website is not intended for children to independently request financial products. Education-loan information concerning a student should involve an appropriate parent/guardian or adult applicant where required.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when our services, technology or applicable requirements change.

12. Contact

Privacy/support email: hellocustomerfirst@gmail.com. For complaints about our assistance, use Support & Grievance.

This website notice does not replace a bank/NBFC/insurer's own privacy notice, Key Facts Statement, sanction terms or grievance process.
