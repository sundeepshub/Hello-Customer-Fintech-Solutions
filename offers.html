Creative placement guide

Hero banner: 1920×600 px for major campaigns.

Campaign banner: 1200×400 px for loan-specific offers.

Offer card: 600×400 px for grid cards.

Small card: 400×300 px where a compact layout is needed.

Keep important text away from image edges, use high-resolution creatives, and include only verified offer/eligibility information. Avoid guarantees such as “100% approval”.
