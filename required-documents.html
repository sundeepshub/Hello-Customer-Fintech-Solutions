Document guidance

Required Documents

Documents vary by loan type, customer profile and lender. This is a general preparation guide, not a final lender checklist.

Common KYC/address

PAN, Aadhaar or other accepted KYC records, address proof, photographs where required and bank statements as requested.

Salaried

Salary slips/certificate, Form 16/26AS where applicable, employment ID, appointment/joining or other employment proof, and bank statements.

Self-employed/business

ITRs, GST/business registration where applicable, bank statements, financial statements, vintage proof and relevant licences/registrations.

Home loan/property

Property/title documents, sale agreement/allotment documents, linked documents, EC where applicable, approved plans/permissions, tax receipts, Gift Deed or Lease Agreement where relevant, and lender-specific legal/technical records.

Existing loans/cards

Loan statements, sanction letters, repayment schedules and current card outstanding/EMI information may be needed for transfer, consolidation or eligibility review.

Never send OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVVs or net-banking credentials.
