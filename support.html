Complaint / Grievance

Email with subject “Grievance – [Enquiry ID]” and include your name, registered mobile, issue, relevant dates and requested resolution.

We will acknowledge and review complaints within a reasonable period based on the nature of the issue.

If the issue concerns a lender

For a bank/NBFC's sanction, pricing, disbursal, repayment, recovery, KFS, charges or regulated service, first use that lender's official grievance channel. Hello Customer cannot alter a lender's final credit decision.

RBI escalation where applicable

For complaints against RBI-regulated entities covered by the applicable RBI Ombudsman framework, escalation may be available after first approaching the regulated entity and satisfying RBI conditions. Use RBI's official Complaint Management System.

Required before public launch: Add the name/designation of your grievance contact and your business correspondence/registered address. If you operate as an LSP/DSA for a regulated entity, publish the lender/RE and grievance disclosures required by your agreement and applicable RBI directions.

Fraud & safety

Do not pay anyone solely for a promise of guaranteed approval and never share authentication credentials.
