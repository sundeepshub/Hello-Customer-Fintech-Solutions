What lenders commonly assess

Eligibility is broader than ROI.

Income & repayment

Income stability, existing EMIs, FOIR/repayment capacity and banking behaviour.

Credit profile

Credit history, overdues, enquiries and lender-internal policy.

Employment / business

Employment stability, business vintage, turnover, GST/ITR/financials where applicable.

Property / project

Title, valuation, LTV, legal/technical checks, approvals and viability where relevant.
